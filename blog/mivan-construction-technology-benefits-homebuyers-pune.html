1. What is Mivan Monolithic Construction?

Originally developed in Malaysia by Mivan Company Ltd in 1990, Mivan construction refers to a structural system where high-strength aluminum formwork panels are assembled to cast both vertical shear walls and horizontal floor slabs simultaneously in a single, continuous concrete pour.

In traditional construction, a developer builds a reinforced concrete (RCC) column-and-beam frame, then lays red clay bricks or Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) blocks between the columns, finishing with multiple layers of sand-cement plaster. This traditional method suffers from inherent structural drawbacks:

  • Mortar Joints & Micro-Cracks: Hundreds of thousands of linear feet of mortar joints that expand and contract with Pune's seasonal temperature swings, causing hair-line plaster cracks.
  • Capillary Seepage: Monsoon rainwater penetrates exterior brick joints, resulting in damp interior paint, mold, and efflorescence.
  • Dimensional Inaccuracy: Human mason error causes uneven room corners, undulating wall plaster, and misaligned door frames.

3. How Mivan Yields 8% to 10% More Usable Carpet Area

In a conventional 2 BHK or 3 BHK apartment, 230mm brick masonry walls occupy substantial square footage inside the unit's boundary. Because Mivan utilizes high-strength M35/M40 grade reinforced concrete, the structural shear walls require only 125mm to 160mm of thickness to achieve even higher load-bearing capacity.

On a 704 sq.ft 2 BHK Premier, this wall thickness difference translates into an additional 55 to 65 sq.ft of pure usable floor area—equivalent to a larger walk-in wardrobe, an extended modular kitchen counter, or a dedicated work-from-home study alcove.

4. Common Myths About Mivan Technology Debunked

Myth 1: "Mivan homes are hotter in summer."

Fact: High-density concrete has higher thermal mass. At Goyal My Home Sanctuary, this is coupled with a 72% preserved forest canopy and 1,000+ native trees, which reduces ambient neighborhood temperatures by 3.2°C, keeping indoor living spaces naturally cooler.

Myth 2: "You cannot drive nails or do carpentry in Mivan walls."

Fact: Standard rotary hammer drills with wall anchors easily mount heavy TV units, overhead kitchen cabinets, and art frames. Pre-engineered electrical conduits are embedded directly inside the formwork during casting.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the lifespan of a Mivan monolithic building?

Mivan monolithic reinforced concrete structures are engineered for an operational design lifespan exceeding 75 to 100 years, significantly outlasting conventional brickwork buildings.

How does Mivan construction affect acoustic noise between neighbors?

Solid reinforced concrete shear walls have higher acoustic density (Sound Transmission Class STC > 50 dB) than hollow AAC blocks, drastically dampening TV sound and conversational noise between neighboring units.
