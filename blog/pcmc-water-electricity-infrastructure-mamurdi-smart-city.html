1. Dispelling Common Misconceptions: PMRDA Gram Panchayat vs PCMC Municipal Corporation

One of the most frequent objections raised by first-time home seekers in Pune West is the fear of purchasing in an "unincorporated gram panchayat zone" where residents must rely permanently on private water tankers and suffer from erratic power blackouts.

It is critical to distinguish between fringe rural zones and Mamurdi:

  • PCMC Municipal Jurisdiction: Mamurdi is an officially gazetted municipal ward of the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC), not an unmanaged Gram Panchayat.
  • Civic Budget Allocation: PCMC has earmarked over ₹450 Crores for the Kiwale-Mamurdi-Gahunje smart infrastructure corridor, funding underground drainage, LED high-mast streetlighting, and 150-ft DP access roads.
  • Town Planning Sanctions: Every building sanction, fire NOC, and commencement certificate is issued directly by PCMC under strict Development Control and Promotion Regulations (DCPR-2020).

3. Electrical Grid Stability & 100% Emergency Backup

Mamurdi benefits from its proximity to the Hinjawadi and Talawade industrial feeder rings, supplied by the Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Company Limited (MSEDCL). Unlike rural areas subject to scheduled load shedding, Mamurdi operates on an express feeder network.

To safeguard against any unforeseen grid fluctuations, Goyal My Home Sanctuary is equipped with:

  1. 100% Diesel Generator (DG) Automation: Auto-start generators engage within 8 seconds of any grid interruption.
  2. Universal Common Area Coverage: All high-speed automatic rescue device (ARD) elevators, fire hydrants, STP pumps, and perimeter CCTV networks remain 100% operational.
  3. In-Flat Designated Power Points: Every residence includes backed-up electrical circuits for Wi-Fi routers, home-office workstations, and primary ambient lighting.

4. Stormwater Drainage & Elevated Basalt Geology

Unlike low-lying areas in Wakad, Hinjawadi Phase 3, or Baner along river flood lines that experience street waterlogging during monsoon deluges, Mamurdi sits upon elevated basalt bedrock with natural contours sloping gently toward the Pavana river basin.

Engineered with subterranean RCC box culverts and roadside storm conduits, Goyal My Home Sanctuary guarantees dry driveways, zero podium water accumulation, and flood-free basement parking even during heavy rainfall events.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What are the water charges under PCMC property tax?

PCMC charges standard metered water tariffs, which are significantly lower than private water tankers (which cost upwards of ₹1,200 to ₹1,800 per 10,000-liter tanker in unregulated areas).

Are cooking gas piped pipelines (PNG) available in the project?

Yes, provision for Maharashtra Natural Gas Limited (MNGL) piped natural gas connections is integrated directly into the kitchen service ducts of each tower.
