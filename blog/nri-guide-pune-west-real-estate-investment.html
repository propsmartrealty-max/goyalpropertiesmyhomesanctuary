2. Regulatory & Legal Framework: FEMA, RBI & MahaRERA

Indian real estate investment has been significantly simplified for diaspora investors:

  • No Prior RBI Approval Needed: NRIs and OCIs can purchase unlimited residential and commercial units in India.
  • Banking Channels: Funds can be routed via standard Non-Resident External (NRE), Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO), or Foreign Currency Non-Resident (FCNR) bank accounts.
  • Full Repatriation: Principal sales proceeds and capital gains can be smoothly repatriated through standard Form 15CA/15CB tax clearances up to USD 1 million per financial year under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS).
  • MahaRERA Statutory Safety: Registered under MahaRERA PR1261012502725 / P52100077438, all buyer funds are deposited into a dedicated 70% project escrow account that can only be withdrawn against certified construction milestones.
