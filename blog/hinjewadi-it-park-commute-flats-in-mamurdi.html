Why Tech Professionals are Moving to Mamurdi

1. The Fast-Track Transit Corridor

Commuting from Mamurdi to Hinjawadi does not require crawling through narrow suburban lanes. By accessing the Dehu Road Katraj-Dehu bypass directly from Mukai Chowk, tech professionals enjoy a wide multi-lane route directly into Bhumkar Chowk and Shivaji Chowk (Phase 1). This ensures that even during peak evening hours, travel times rarely exceed 25 to 30 minutes.

2. ₹15 to ₹25 Lakh Financial Savings

A typical 2 BHK apartment in Wakad or Hinjawadi Phase 1 costs upwards of ₹85 Lakhs to ₹98 Lakhs all-inclusive. At Goyal My Home Sanctuary, an equivalent 2 BHK residence with superior 100% monolithic ALUFORM Mivan construction begins at just ₹69 Lakhs*. That difference represents ₹16,000 to ₹22,000 saved on monthly home loan EMIs, or capital that can be deployed into child education or equity investments.

3. Hybrid Work & Co-Working Infrastructure

With modern tech companies operating on hybrid models (2-3 days work from home), residential quality of life has surpassed mere distance. Goyal My Home Sanctuary features dedicated soundproof co-working suites, private video-conferencing booths, and high-speed enterprise optical fiber integrated throughout the 35,000 sq.ft clubhouse. You can execute global product calls without domestic background noise.

4. Mental Rejuvenation in a 26-Acre Biophilic Sanctuary

High-stress tech careers require calm living environments. Goyal My Home Sanctuary is intentionally planned around 72% preserved forest canopy with a 1.2 km nature walking spine, yoga pavilion, temperature-regulated lap pool, and professional pickleball courts. Returning home feels like retreating to a luxury resort outside the city's concrete heat island.
