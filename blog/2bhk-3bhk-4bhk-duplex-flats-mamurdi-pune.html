An architectural dissection of how MIVAN monolithic construction enables 98% usable carpet efficiency, zero column intrusions, and seamless sky duplex integrations at Goyal My Home Sanctuary.

1. Architectural Philosophy: The Biophilic Floor Plan

Most high-density developments in Pune suffer from what architects term "compromised floor geometry": narrow internal passages that eat up 10-15% of your carpet space, master bedrooms abutting noisy lift shafts, and cramped balconies that barely hold two chairs.

At Goyal My Home Sanctuary, the architectural brief prioritized three immutable design rules:

  • Zero Column Intrusion: Monolithic MIVAN cast-in-place concrete walls replace cumbersome protruding RCC columns, allowing flush furniture placement and clean interior lines.
  • Dual-Orientation Cross Ventilation: Every home enjoys an open breezeway channel, pulling cooler air from the 72% tree canopy into bedrooms and living lounges.
  • Dedicated Functional Zoning: Formal living areas, dining alcoves, and private sleep quarters are acoustically separated for privacy.
