1. Why NMIAL Replaces CSIA as Pune's De Facto International Airport

For decades, international travelers and business executives residing in Pune faced a gruelling journey to reach Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (CSIA) in Santacruz. Navigating Sion-Panvel Highway, Chedda Nagar bottlenecks, and the Western Express Highway routinely added 2 to 3 hours of urban stop-and-go congestion.

The commissioning of the Navi Mumbai International Airport (NMIAL) developed by Adani Airport Holdings changes everything. Positioned adjacent to the Panvel expressway exit, Pune motorists exit the Mumbai-Pune Expressway directly into airport approach roads, completely avoiding Mumbai's city core.

2. The Gateway Effect: Real Estate Capital Flow into Mamurdi

Historically in global metropolitan regions, the closest master-planned residential cluster situated on an access-controlled expressway to an international airport captures outsized capital appreciation (e.g., Gurgaon near Delhi IGI Airport, Devanahalli near Bengaluru Airport).

In Pune, that premier gateway is Mamurdi. With the confluence of:

  • The Mumbai-Pune Expressway terminal toll plaza at Mukai Chowk
  • The MSRDC Missing Link bypass tunnel
  • Direct connectivity to the upcoming Pune Ring Road
