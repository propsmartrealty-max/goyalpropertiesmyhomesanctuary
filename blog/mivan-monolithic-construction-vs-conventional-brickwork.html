MIVAN Monolithic Aluminum Formwork vs Conventional Brickwork: Engineering Breakdown

Why Goyal Properties engineered all 14 G+28 high-rise residential towers at Goyal My Home Sanctuary in 100% cast-in-place monolithic concrete—delivering lifetime structural integrity.

1. The Engineering Paradigm: Monolithic vs Infill Construction

In conventional high-rise construction, an RCC skeleton (columns and beams) is erected first, followed by manual brick or AAC block infill walls laid with cement mortar. This hybrid method creates millions of micro-joints between structural concrete and masonry, which become vulnerable to differential thermal expansion, hairline plaster cracks, and external rainwater seepage over time.

At Goyal My Home Sanctuary, the engineering team deployed MIVAN Monolithic Aluminum Formwork. Entire floor plates—including perimeter shear walls, internal partition walls, floor slabs, staircases, and sun decks—are poured simultaneously in high-strength, self-compacting concrete.

3. Environmental & Thermal Insulation Benefits

High-grade monolithic concrete combined with the township's 72% native bio-canopy creates a micro-climate buffer. Ambient indoor temperatures at Goyal My Home Sanctuary remain significantly cooler during summer peaks, reducing household air conditioning electricity consumption by an estimated 15%–20%.
