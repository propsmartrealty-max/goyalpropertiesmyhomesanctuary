1. Itemized Cost Breakdown: 7.0% Total Stamp Duty

Under the Maharashtra Stamp Act, residential properties purchased within the jurisdictional limits of the Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC) or Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) are subject to a 3-part statutory levy:

  • Base Stamp Duty (5.0%): Standard state revenue component applied on the higher of Agreement Value or Annual Statement of Rates (Ready Reckoner).
  • Local Body Tax / Urban Surcharge (1.0%): Allocated to the municipal corporation for urban infrastructure maintenance.
  • Metro Cess (1.0%): Dedicated transport surcharge levied across Pune Metropolitan Region to fund the expansion of Pune Metro Line 1, 2, and 3.
