2. The Civil Court Multi-Modal Interchange Advantage

The eastern terminus at Civil Court functions as the epicentral multi-modal hub connecting Line 3 with Line 1 (PCMC to Swargate) and Line 2 (Vanaz to Ramwadi). For professionals commuting from West Pune, this allows seamless one-ticket transfers to Pune Railway Station, Swargate bus terminal, and the airport feeder corridors.
